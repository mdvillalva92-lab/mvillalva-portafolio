Professional Consulting

Operational excellence requires more than just data; it requires clinical context. The following case studies illustrate my work with renal care facilities, where I aligned medical safety standards with business efficiency to reduce downtime and ensure ISO 9001 compliance.

Villamedica: Clinical Process Optimization

SCOPE: Process standardization and quality improvement for renal service operations.

KEY ACHIEVEMENTS:

  • KPI Design: Implemented monitoring routines for equipment reliability and patient safety metrics.
  • Compliance: Supported alignment with ISO 9001:2015 quality standards, preparing the facility for external audits.
  • Workflow: Redesigned operational documentation to reduce errors and improve staff onboarding.

Focus: Quality Assurance, Process Mapping, Safety.

Renalcoca: Risk Mitigation & Resource Strategy

SCOPE: Operational assessment and improvement roadmap for dialysis support processes.

KEY ACHIEVEMENTS:

Academic Projects (MBA)

Leveraging advanced business analytics to solve critical operational challenges in healthcare. These projects demonstrate my proficiency in capacity modeling, digital process automation, and the strategic evaluation of AI technologies

SGH Bed Management System

Capacity Planning & Patient Flow Optimization

Context: A tertiary hospital faced bottlenecks in bed allocation, leading to ER overcrowding.

  • My Contribution: Modeled patient demand, discharge constraints, and surge scenarios using data simulation.
  • Outcome: Proposed new triage rules and coordination protocols expected to reduce bed turnover time.
  • Tools: Excel modeling, Queuing Theory, Process Mapping.


Digital Transformation in Pharma: Pfizer Case

Pharmacovigilance Automation with Atlassian Rovo

Context: Designed a digital transformation prototype for Pfizer’s pharmacovigilance unit to manage adverse event reports.

My Contribution:

  • Jira Automation: Configured a Kanban workflow to track adverse events (e.g., Anaphylaxis, Cold Chain failures).
  • AI Implementation: Programmed Atlassian Rovo Agents to analyze issue descriptions and automatically assign severity levels.
  • Compliance: Created rules for auto-generating regulatory closure reports using Generative AI.

Outcome: A fully functional simulation reducing manual triage time and ensuring standardized ISO/FDA compliance documentation.

Tools: Jira Automation, Atlassian Intelligence, Rovo Agents.

LLM Benchmarking in Healthcare Operations

Comparative Analysis of AI Models for Hospital Decision-Making

Context: Evaluated the reliability of Large Language Models (LLMs) for supporting operational decisions in government hospitals, focusing on bias and ethical alignment.

My Contribution:

  • Experimental Design: Created a framework comparing Human expert responses against 4 major LLMs (ChatGPT-4, Gemini, Claude, Llama) across 10 operational scenarios.
  • Ethical Auditing: Specifically assessed cultural sensitivity (Indigenous inclusion) and safety biases in procurement and resource allocation.
  • Statistical Analysis: Analyzed volatility and accuracy, finding that Claude and Gemini offered the highest stability in LLM.

Outcome: Recommended a Hybrid Governance Model where AI augments but does not replace human oversight in critical patient safety decisions.

Tools: Prompt Engineering, Comparative Analysis, Statistical Reporting
